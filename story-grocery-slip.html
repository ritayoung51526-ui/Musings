The Grocery Store Ice Rink

Short · ~4 min Supermarket Life · The Wet Floor Incident
Dairy Aisle · The Unexpected Slide

I had just finished a full shift at work, and all I wanted from the world was to grab a carton of milk and go home. I did not, at any point, plan on becoming a cautionary tale for grocery store safety posters everywhere.

我剛結束一整天的工作，此刻對這個世界唯一的要求，就是買一盒牛奶然後回家。我完全沒有計劃，要在這個過程中，變成超市安全宣傳海報上的活生生反面教材。

Somewhere near the dairy section, a small yellow "wet floor" sign stood at a jaunty angle, half-hidden behind a display of yogurt cups. I did not see it. I was too busy checking my grocery list on my phone, walking at my usual brisk, purposeful pace.

在乳製品區附近，一塊小小的黃色「地板濕滑」警示牌，被一堆優格展示架半遮著，歪歪斜斜地立在那裡。而我完全沒有看見它。我當時正忙著低頭看手機上的購物清單，用一貫俐落、目標明確的步伐快步前進。

What happened next took approximately one and a half seconds, but in my memory it plays out in agonizing slow motion. My right foot hit the wet patch. My leg shot forward like it belonged to someone else entirely.

接下來發生的事，實際上大概只花了一秒半的時間，但在我的記憶裡，卻是用一種令人痛苦的慢動作重播著。我的右腳踩上了那片濕滑的地面。我的那條腿瞬間向前滑出去，彷彿根本不屬於我自己。

My arms windmilled through the air in a desperate attempt to stay upright. For one glorious, hopeful half-second, I actually thought I might pull it off, might casually recover like nothing had happened. I did not pull it off.

我的雙臂在空中瘋狂揮舞，拚命想維持身體平衡。有那麼輝煌、充滿希望的半秒鐘，我真心以為自己或許能挺過去，可以雲淡風輕地恢復站姿，彷彿什麼事都沒發生過。我沒能挺過去。

I landed flat on my back with a sound that echoed through the entire aisle—a sort of dull, full-body thud, immediately followed by the sound of a full carton of eggs I had been carrying, hitting the floor beside me and cracking open with a wet splat.

我整個人仰面朝天摔在地上，發出的聲響迴盪整條走道——那是一種悶悶的、全身撞擊的巨響，緊接著是我手裡原本拿著的一整盒雞蛋摔落在我身旁，「啪」一聲裂開的濕黏聲音。

For a second, I just lay there, staring up at the fluorescent supermarket lights, feeling a strange, detached calmness, as if this were happening to a stranger and I was simply a curious observer.

有那麼一秒，我只是躺在原地，望著超市頭頂那盞日光燈，心裡湧上一種奇怪的、抽離事外的平靜感，彷彿這一切正發生在某個陌生人身上，而我只是個湊巧路過、好奇圍觀的旁觀者。

Then reality caught up with me, along with three concerned strangers and a teenage store employee who rushed over, asking if I was okay while trying very, very hard not to laugh. I was not okay. My dignity had left the building.

然後，現實感終於追上了我，連同三位擔心的陌生人，以及一位急忙衝過來的年輕店員，一邊問我有沒有事，一邊拚了命地忍著不笑出聲。我當然有事。我的尊嚴，已經徹底離開了這棟建築物。

I got up, insisted—loudly and unconvincingly—that I was completely fine, and walked briskly to the checkout with a soggy sleeve, a bruised elbow, and absolutely no eggs. To this day, I read every single "wet floor" sign like my life depends on it.

我爬起身，大聲又毫無說服力地堅稱自己完全沒事，然後帶著濕透的袖子、瘀青的手肘、以及一顆雞蛋都不剩的購物籃，快步走向收銀台。時至今日，我看到任何一塊「地板濕滑」的警示牌，都會像攸關性命一樣認真讀完。
