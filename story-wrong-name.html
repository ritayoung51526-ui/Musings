The Wrong Name

Short · ~4 min School & Classroom · Cringe Fail
Classroom · The Raised Hand Incident

It was third period AP Literature, and the classroom was so quiet you could hear the fluorescent lights buzzing overhead. Mr. Henderson had just asked a question about the green light in The Great Gatsby, and approximately twenty-eight teenagers were suddenly fascinated by the wood grain on their desks, praying not to make eye contact.

那是第三節的大學先修文學課，教室裡安靜得連頭頂日光燈的微弱嗡嗡聲都聽得一清二楚。亨德森老師剛剛拋出了一個關於《大亨小傳》中綠燈象徵意義的問題，全班大約二十八名高中生頓時像被施了定身咒一樣，全神貫注地盯著桌上的木紋，暗自祈禱千萬不要與老師眼神交會。

I, however, had actually read the assigned chapter. I’d spent two hours the night before highlighting themes, looking up literary devices, and practically writing an essay in the margins. When Mr. Henderson sighed and leaned against the podium, scanning the room for volunteers, an unfamiliar surge of courage took over. My right hand shot straight up into the air, perfectly rigid, radiating pure, unadulterated confidence.

然而，我昨晚確實讀完了指定的章節。我花了整整兩個小時劃重點、查閱修辭手法，甚至在書頁邊緣密密麻麻寫滿了筆記。當亨德森老師嘆了口氣、靠在講台上環視全班尋找自願者時，一股平日罕見的勇氣突然湧上心頭。我的右手猛地筆直舉起，手臂繃得緊緊的，渾身散發著毫無雜質的自信。

Mr. Henderson’s face lit up. He pointed his dry-erase marker directly in my direction, gave a warm, encouraging smile, and said clearly: “Yes, Toby! Thank you.”

亨德森老師眼前一亮。他手中的白板筆直直指向我這個方向，臉上露出了溫和而欣慰的笑容，聲音洪亮地說：「很好，托比！謝謝你主動。」

For three agonizing seconds, my brain malfunctioned. I kept my hand frozen in the air, grinning eagerly at the whiteboard like a golden retriever waiting for a tennis ball. I am not Toby. I have never been Toby. Toby was the six-foot-two varsity soccer midfielder sitting directly behind me, currently wearing an oversized gray hoodie, slumped over his desk fast asleep with his forehead planted firmly on his binder.

在極其痛苦的三秒鐘裡，我的大腦徹底當機了。我的手依然僵硬地懸在半空中，滿臉堆笑地看著黑板，活像一隻殷切等待拋球的黃金獵犬。問題是，我不叫托比，我也從來都不是托比。托比是坐在我正後方、身高一米八八的校隊足球中場，此時他正套著一件寬大的灰色連帽衫，整個人癱倒在課桌上，額頭死死抵在活頁夾上睡得不省人事。

Mr. Henderson wasn't calling on a volunteer. He was trying to catch Toby sleeping.

亨德森老師根本不是在點自願回答問題的人，他只是想抓現行，把上課睡覺的托比叫醒。

The realization rippled through the classroom in slow motion. The girl in the second row snorted into her hydro flask. Two guys near the window exchanged wide-eyed glances and started shaking with muffled laughter. Meanwhile, my arm was still extended toward the ceiling, looking like an antenna trying to pick up signals from outer space.

這一殘酷的真相像慢動作一樣在教室裡蔓延開來。第二排的女生一口水噴在保溫瓶裡，嗆出了聲。靠窗的兩個男生瞪大雙眼面面相覷，隨後肩膀劇烈抖動，極力忍住狂笑。與此同時，我的手臂依然孤零零地伸向天花板，活像一根試圖接收外太空訊號的天線。

Mr. Henderson paused, looked at my eager, solitary hand, and then looked at the sleeping lump behind me. His eyebrows twitched with mild embarrassment. “Oh—sorry Maya,” he cleared his throat. “I was just checking if Toby was still biologically alive. But... since your hand is already halfway to the ceiling, why don’t you take it away?”

亨德森老師愣住了。他先是看了看我孤零零高舉的手，又看了看我身後那團睡成一灘的泥偶，眉頭略帶尷尬地抽搐了一下。「呃——抱歉，瑪雅，」他清了清嗓子，「我剛才只是想確認托比是否還有生命跡象。不過……既然你的手都快舉到天花板了，不如就由你來回答吧？」

A wave of blistering heat climbed from the collar of my sweater straight to the tips of my ears. I pulled my arm down so fast my shoulder made an audible pop. “The... the green light represents Gatsby's unattainable dreams,” I managed to squeak out. My voice cracked on the word unattainable, giving it a delightful adolescent squeal that echoed off the whiteboards.

一股滾燙的熱浪瞬間從我的毛衣領口直竄耳尖。我把手縮回來的速度快得連肩膀關節都發出了一聲清脆的輕響。「那、那個綠燈……代表蓋茨比無法觸及的夢想，」我勉強擠出一句話。在唸到「無法觸及」這個詞時，我的聲音甚至尷尬地破了音，在教室裡留下了一聲清脆的青春期怪叫。

“Very insightful,” Mr. Henderson said gently, trying his best not to smile. Behind me, Toby finally stirred, blinked groggily at the fluorescent lights, and muttered, “Wait, did we have homework?” That sent the entire back row into uncontrolled hysterics.

「非常深刻的見解，」亨德森老師盡量忍住笑意，溫和地說道。而我身後那個睡神托比這時才揉著惺忪睡眼醒過來，茫然地看著刺眼的日光燈，嘟囔了一句：「等等，今天有功課嗎？」這句話頓時引得後排所有男生爆發出一陣失控的狂笑。

For the remaining thirty-five minutes, I kept my eyes glued to page 112, wishing I had the power of spontaneous invisibility. When the final bell rang, I shoved my notebook into my backpack without even checking if the zipper caught and vanished into the hallway crowd within four seconds flat.

在接下來的三十五分鐘裡，我的視線死死黏在課本第一百一十二頁上，恨不得自己當場覺醒隱形超能力。放學鐘聲敲響的那一刻，我連拉鍊有沒有咬住內襯都顧不上，把筆記本一股腦塞進書包，四秒鐘內便徹底消失在走廊洶湧的人潮之中。

It’s been months now. Toby still doesn't know who Gatsby is, and Mr. Henderson has never mixed up our names again. But occasionally, when I’m lying in bed at 2:15 AM unable to sleep, my brain kindly replays the footage in crisp 4K resolution: me, beaming proudly, answering with my entire heart to the name Toby.

這件事已經過去好幾個月了。托比依然不知道蓋茨比是何方神聖，而亨德森老師也再沒有叫錯過我們的名字。但偶爾在凌晨兩點十五分失眠的夜裡，我的大腦依然會非常貼心地以 4K 超高清畫質在眼前重播那一幕：我滿臉驕傲、滿懷熱忱，歡天喜地替一個叫托比的男生舉手應答。
