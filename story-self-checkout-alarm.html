The Self-Checkout Alarm

Short · ~4 min Shopping Life · The Machine That Accused Me
Self-Checkout Lane · Unexpected Item in the Bagging Area

I have always chosen self-checkout over a regular cashier for one simple reason: I do not enjoy small talk while purchasing thirty dollars' worth of snack food. Machines don't judge you. Or so I believed, before that Thursday evening.

我一直偏好使用自助結帳，而不是找真人收銀員，理由很單純：我不喜歡在購買價值三十美元的零食時，還要應付閒聊。機器不會對你評頭論足——至少在那個星期四晚上之前，我一直是這麼相信的。

I was scanning items at a fairly efficient pace, sliding each one across the reader and setting it neatly into the bagging area. A box of cereal, a jar of peanut butter, a bag of oranges. So far, so ordinary.

我用一種頗有效率的節奏掃描著商品，把每樣東西滑過感應器，再整整齊齊放進裝袋區。一盒穀片、一罐花生醬、一袋橘子。到目前為止，一切都稀鬆平常。

Then I scanned a bag of frozen dumplings, and before I could even reach to place it in the bagging area, my elbow accidentally knocked my reusable tote bag off the edge of the counter and onto the scale below.

接著我掃了一包冷凍水餃，還沒來得及伸手把它放進裝袋區，我的手肘就不小心把我自己帶的環保購物袋，從櫃檯邊緣撞落到下方的秤重感應區上。

The machine did not appreciate this. A red light began flashing furiously above my station, and a robotic voice announced, at a volume that seemed specifically calibrated to humiliate, "UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA."

這台機器對此顯然一點也不領情。我這台結帳機上方的紅燈開始瘋狂閃爍，一個機器人聲音以一種彷彿專門校準來羞辱人的音量宣告：「裝袋區出現異常物品。」

I quickly removed my bag, assuming that would fix it. It did not fix it. The light kept flashing. The voice repeated its accusation, louder this time, as if the machine had decided I was personally trying to steal a bag of frozen dumplings.

我趕緊把袋子拿開，以為這樣就能解決問題。並沒有解決。紅燈依舊瘋狂閃爍，那個聲音再次發出指控，這次還更大聲了，彷彿這台機器已經認定，我是存心要偷走一包冷凍水餃。

Other shoppers began glancing over, that particular mix of curiosity and secondhand embarrassment on their faces. A staff member finally wandered over with the unhurried pace of someone who had seen this happen roughly four hundred times before.

周圍的其他購物客紛紛投來視線，臉上帶著一種好奇與替我尷尬並存的表情。終於，一位店員以一種不慌不忙、彷彿早已見過四百次同樣狀況的從容步調，緩緩晃了過來。

She tapped a few buttons on a small key attached to a lanyard, and the alarm finally, mercifully, went silent. "Happens all the time," she said flatly, not even looking up, already walking away before I could finish stammering out a thank you.

她用掛在頸繩上的一把小鑰匙按了幾個按鈕，那個警報聲終於，謝天謝地，安靜了下來。「這種事常發生，」她語氣平淡地說道，連頭都沒抬，我還沒把結結巴巴的謝謝說完，她就已經轉身走遠了。

I finished checking out in record time after that, refusing to make eye contact with a single person in that store. To this day, I place my bags on the far end of the counter, as far from that scale as physically possible.

在那之後，我用破紀錄的速度結完帳，全程拒絕和店裡任何一個人有眼神接觸。時至今日，我結帳時都會把袋子放在櫃檯最遠的一端，離那個秤重感應區越遠越好。
