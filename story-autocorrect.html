The Autocorrect Catastrophe

Short · ~4 min Texting Life · The Wrong Word at the Worst Time
Group Chat · The Autocorrect Ambush

I type fast. Too fast, if I'm being honest, which means I rely on autocorrect the way some people rely on caffeine—blindly, completely, and without ever really reading the results before hitting send.

我打字速度很快。老實說，快到有點誇張，這代表我對自動校正的依賴，就跟有些人依賴咖啡因一樣——盲目、徹底，而且從來不會在按下送出前，真正檢查一遍打出來的內容。

I was in two separate chats that afternoon: one with my best friend Mia, where we were planning a surprise for her birthday, and one with my new supervisor, Ms. Whitfield, about rescheduling a client meeting.

那天下午我同時開著兩個對話視窗：一個是跟我最好的朋友米亞聊天，正在籌劃她的生日驚喜；另一個則是跟我剛上任不久的主管惠特菲爾德女士，討論如何重新安排一場客戶會議。

I meant to tell Mia, "the cake is going to be a total disaster, I can't cook to save my life." My thumbs moved fast, autocorrect quietly did its own thing in the background, and I hit send without glancing back at the screen.

我本來想跟米亞說：「這個蛋糕肯定會是一場大災難，我這輩子都不會做飯。」我的手指飛快地打字，自動校正則在背景默默地做著自己的事，而我送出訊息前，連螢幕都沒有回頭看一眼。

Except my thumb had tapped the wrong chat thread entirely. The message about the disastrous cake and my complete inability to cook went, in full, to Ms. Whitfield. About our client meeting. With zero context whatsoever.

結果我的手指根本點錯了對話視窗。那則關於災難蛋糕、以及我完全不會做飯的訊息，一字不差地發給了惠特菲爾德女士。而那個對話串，原本是在討論客戶會議。完全沒有任何前後文可言。

Three minutes of silence followed. Excruciating, soul-crushing silence. Then my phone buzzed with her reply: "I'm not sure how this relates to Thursday's meeting, but... good luck with the cake?"

接下來是三分鐘的沉默。那是一種折磨人、幾乎能把靈魂壓垮的沉默。然後我的手機震動了一下，是她的回覆：「我不太確定這跟星期四的會議有什麼關聯，不過……祝你的蛋糕順利？」

I felt a wave of pure mortification wash over me. I quickly typed out a panicked apology, explaining it was meant for a friend, my fingers trembling so badly I autocorrected two more words in the apology itself.

一股純粹的羞愧感瞬間淹沒了我。我慌張地打出道歉訊息，解釋那則訊息本來是要傳給朋友的，而我的手指抖得太厲害，結果連這則道歉訊息裡，又被自動校正改錯了兩個字。

To my relief, Ms. Whitfield turned out to have a good sense of humor about it. Her next message simply read: "No worries at all. For what it's worth, disaster cakes taste the same as good ones after enough frosting."

讓我鬆一口氣的是，惠特菲爾德女士原來還挺幽默的。她的下一則訊息只寫著：「完全沒關係。不過老實跟你說，只要糖霜夠多，災難蛋糕吃起來跟成功的蛋糕根本沒兩樣。」

Mia, meanwhile, never received the message at all and texted me an hour later asking if I'd forgotten about the cake entirely. I now, without exception, triple-check every single text before sending it—especially to anyone with "supervisor" in their contact name.

而米亞那邊呢，則根本沒收到那則訊息，一個小時後還傳訊息問我，是不是把蛋糕的事給忘得一乾二淨。從那天起，我發任何訊息前都毫無例外要檢查三遍——尤其是聯絡人名稱裡有「主管」兩個字的對象。
