The Wrong Wave

Short · ~4 min Street Life · Mistaken Identity
Bus Stop · The Enthusiastic Wave Fail

I am, at heart, a very friendly person. If I recognize someone across the street, I will wave. Not a shy little half-wave either—a big, full-arm, both-eyebrows-raised, genuinely delighted wave. This has served me well for most of my life. Most of my life.

骨子裡，我是個非常熱情的人。只要在馬路對面認出熟人，我一定會揮手打招呼。而且不是那種害羞的半吊子揮手，是那種整隻手臂大幅度擺動、雙眉高高揚起、真心喜悅的揮手。這個習慣多半時候都為我帶來好人緣。多半時候。

One afternoon, waiting at the bus stop, I spotted a familiar figure across the street—same haircut, same navy jacket, same slightly hunched walk as my old university roommate, Daniel, whom I hadn't seen in almost two years.

某天下午，我站在巴士站等車，瞥見馬路對面站著一個熟悉的身影——同樣的髮型、同樣的深藍色外套、同樣有點駝背的走路姿勢，簡直就是我那位快兩年沒見的大學室友丹尼爾。

Without a second thought, I broke into a huge grin, threw both arms into the air, and started waving like I was signaling a rescue helicopter. "DANIEL!" I shouted across four lanes of traffic. "OH MY GOSH, IT'S BEEN FOREVER!"

我想都沒想，臉上立刻綻開一個大大的笑容，雙臂高舉過頭，開始瘋狂揮手，彷彿在向救援直升機發出訊號一樣。「丹尼爾！」我隔著四線車道大喊：「天啊，真的好久不見了！」

The man turned around, and my stomach did a slow, sinking somersault. It was not Daniel. It was a complete stranger, perhaps in his fifties, wearing an expression of deep and understandable bewilderment, glancing over both shoulders to see who I could possibly be shouting at.

那個男人轉過頭來，我的胃緩緩地、徹底地往下一沉。那根本不是丹尼爾。那是個完全陌生的男人，大概五十多歲，臉上帶著一種深刻且完全合理的困惑神情，還回頭左右張望，想確認我到底是在對誰大喊大叫。

I kept waving for a solid two more seconds, my brain refusing to accept reality, before it finally caught up with my eyes. By then it was too late to just casually stop. My arm was already mid-air, frozen like a broken windmill.

我又硬是多揮了整整兩秒鐘，腦袋還在拒絕接受現實，直到理智終於追上了雙眼看見的畫面。但那時要優雅地假裝沒事已經來不及了。我的手臂還高舉在半空中，僵在那裡，像一座壞掉的風車。

In a moment of pure panic, instead of just admitting the mistake, I did something far worse—I pointed at him, gave two enthusiastic thumbs up, and yelled, "GREAT JACKET!" as if that had been my plan all along.

在那個純粹恐慌的瞬間，我沒有選擇坦然承認自己認錯人，反而做了一件更糟糕的事——我指著他，豎起兩根大拇指用力比讚，並大喊：「外套好帥！」彷彿這一切從頭到尾都是我的計畫一樣。

The stranger just stared at me for a long moment, gave a slow, uncertain nod as if humoring a lunatic, and quickly crossed to the other side of the street, walking noticeably faster than before.

那位陌生人只是盯著我看了好長一段時間，緩緩地、帶著猶疑地點了點頭，彷彿在敷衍一個瘋子，然後迅速穿過馬路走到另一邊，步伐明顯比剛才快了不少。

My actual bus arrived thirty seconds later. I got on, sat by the window, and spent the entire ride replaying the words "great jacket" in my head, wondering if there was any universe where that had sounded normal. There wasn't.

我真正要搭的巴士在三十秒後抵達。我上了車，坐在窗邊，整趟車程都在腦海裡不斷重播「外套好帥」這句話，反覆思考在哪個平行宇宙裡這句話聽起來會顯得正常。答案是：沒有這樣的宇宙。
