The Unmuted Meeting

Short · ~4 min Work From Home · The Microphone Betrayal
Video Call · The Mute Button Fail

Working from home had made me extremely confident about one thing: my ability to tell, at a glance, whether my microphone was muted. That little red icon in the corner of the screen had become my most trusted companion. Or so I thought.

在家工作讓我對一件事變得異常自信：我一眼就能判斷麥克風有沒有靜音。螢幕角落那個小小的紅色圖示，早已成了我最信賴的夥伴。至少，我當時是這麼以為的。

It was a Monday morning client call, the important kind, with twelve people from three different companies staring at their own little video boxes. My manager was presenting the quarterly numbers. I had, of course, muted myself the second the call started, like a responsible professional.

那是一場週一早上的客戶會議，還是重要的那種，來自三間不同公司的十二個人，正盯著自己那個小小的視訊方塊。我的主管正在報告季度數字。而我，理所當然地，在會議一開始就把自己靜音了，就像個負責任的專業人士。

Except, unbeknownst to me, my cat had just knocked a glass of water directly onto my desk, and in the confusion of grabbing towels, my elbow must have brushed the mute icon back on. I had no idea. I was blissfully, catastrophically unaware.

只是我完全不知道，我家的貓咪剛剛把一杯水打翻在我的書桌上，而在我手忙腳亂抓毛巾擦水的混亂中，我的手肘八成不小心把靜音又碰開了。我對此毫不知情，處於一種幸福而災難性的渾然不覺之中。

As I mopped up the puddle, I muttered under my breath, in what I believed was total privacy, "Oh my god, why does he always talk for twenty minutes when five would do, I'm going to lose my mind in this meeting."

在我擦拭那攤水漬的同時，我低聲自言自語，自以為完全沒有人聽得到：「天啊，明明五分鐘就能講完，他為什麼每次都要講二十分鐘，這場會議真的快把我逼瘋了。」

The meeting went eerily silent. My manager, mid-sentence about quarterly revenue, simply stopped talking. Twelve tiny video boxes all seemed to freeze at once. I looked up from the towel in my hand, confused by the sudden quiet.

會議室瞬間陷入一種詭異的死寂。我的主管，原本正講到一半的季度營收，突然停了下來。十二個小小的視訊方塊，彷彿同時被按下了暫停鍵。我從手裡的毛巾抬起頭，對這突如其來的安靜感到一頭霧水。

Then I saw it. My icon, unmuted, glowing a bright, undeniable green. My manager's voice came back through my speakers, dry as sandpaper. "Please continue," he said, "I'd love to hear the rest of that thought."

然後我看到了。我的圖示，並沒有靜音，正閃爍著一種明亮、無可辯駁的綠色。我主管的聲音透過喇叭傳來，乾得像砂紙一樣：「請繼續，」他說：「我很想聽聽你接下來要說什麼。」

I have never hit a mute button faster in my life. My face burned so hot I was genuinely worried it might set off my smoke detector. I typed an apology in the chat with shaking fingers while my cat, completely unbothered, sat licking the spilled water off the desk.

我這輩子從沒按靜音按過那麼快。我的臉燙得厲害，我甚至真心擔心會不會觸發家裡的煙霧警報器。我顫抖著手指在聊天室裡打字道歉，而我家的貓，完全事不關己，正坐在書桌上舔著剛剛灑出來的水。

Somehow, my manager let it slide with just a tired chuckle, and the meeting moved on. But for weeks afterward, whenever he did go on a bit long in a call, he'd glance at the camera and say, with a small smirk, "Feel free to unmute if you have thoughts."

不知怎地，我的主管只是疲憊地笑了一聲便放過了我，會議也繼續進行下去。但接下來好幾個星期，只要他在會議上講得稍微久了一點，就會瞄一眼鏡頭，帶著一絲不易察覺的壞笑說：「有想法的話，隨時歡迎解除靜音。」
